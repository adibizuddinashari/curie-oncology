About Us

Our story

Background and history

Curie Oncology is named in honour of Marie Curie, the pioneering scientist whose discovery of radium laid the foundation for modern radiotherapy, and the first person to win two Nobel Prizes.

Our identity was conceived as a tribute to her legacy, carried by our tagline, Spring will come. In nature’s cycle of seasons, spring brings warmth and new beginnings. For us, it stands for hope and the promise of better days ahead.

Why Curie was established

“Our multidisciplinary team takes the time to understand each patient, tailoring treatment to their needs and walking alongside them through every step of their journey…”

At Curie Oncology, we believe cancer care should be as individual as the people we treat. As a boutique practice specialising in complex cases, our multidisciplinary team takes the time to understand each patient, tailoring treatment to their needs and walking alongside them through every step of their journey towards recovery and renewed life.

Our philosophy

Personalised cancer care

Curie’s philosophy of personalised cancer care

  • Innovation in care

    We embrace new technology early so our patients can benefit from advanced diagnostics and treatment, giving each person the best possible foundation for their care.

  • Research for a better future

    We believe research holds the key to defeating cancer. That’s why Curie Oncology supports and undertakes research to improve how cancer is diagnosed, treated and prevented, working towards a day when no one has to live in fear of cancer.

Our network

Regional reach

One team across borders

Curie Malaysia consultants work closely with Curie Singapore’s seven specialists in medical oncology, radiation oncology and haematology.

One team with Curie Singapore

Subspecialty specialists

  • Gastrointestinal

Bi-weekly knowledge sharing
