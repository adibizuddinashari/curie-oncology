About Dr Tan Ai Lian

Dr Tan is a Clinical Oncologist at Curie Penang, with ten years as a specialist in Malaysian public hospitals, leading cancer trials in Johor Bahru and Penang.

Dr Tan previously practised as Clinical Oncologist at Hospital Pulau Pinang (2018–2026) and as visiting oncologist at Hospital Alor Setar, and before that at Hospital Sultan Ismail, Johor Bahru (2016–2018).

From 2006 to 2015, Dr Tan volunteered as a doctor at the Tzu Chi, Amata and Mitta free and cancer support clinics.

Cancer types managed

Lung, head and neck (including nasopharyngeal) and gastrointestinal cancers. Also treats hepatobiliary and breast cancers.

Clinical interests

  • Principal Investigator in 25 industry-sponsored trials, 16 of them in lung cancer
  • Published on IMRT for nasopharyngeal cancer and chemotherapy safety (2012–2014)
