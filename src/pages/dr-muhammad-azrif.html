About Dr Muhammad Azrif

Dr Muhammad Azrif Bin Ahmad Annuar joins Curie Oncology as a Visiting Senior Consultant Clinical Oncologist, with a clinical focus on radiation oncology. He completed his MBBS at the Royal Free and University College Medical School, University of London, in 1997, before earning his MRCP in 2000 and his FRCR in 2005.

He undertook a Clinical Research Fellowship at the Department of Radiation Oncology, University of Toronto, Canada, in 2007, following a Specialist Registrar post in Clinical Oncology at the Christie Hospital, Manchester, UK, from 2001 to 2006. He was admitted to the UK General Medical Council's Specialist Register in 2006.

Dr Azrif has practised as a Consultant Clinical Oncologist at Prince Court Medical Centre and Gleneagles Hospital Kuala Lumpur since 2012, and previously at UKM Medical Centre from 2007 to 2012. He served as President of the Malaysian Oncological Society from 2018 to 2021, and is a widely published contributor to oncology journals and research.
