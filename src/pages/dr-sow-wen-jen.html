About Dr Jenson Sow Wen Jen

Dr Jenson is a Consultant Clinical Oncologist with expertise across systemic therapy and advanced radiotherapy, certified as a Clinical Oncologist under the National Specialist Register.

He has served as Principal Investigator and Sub-Investigator in international multicentre oncology trials, including six international trials of new treatments for lung, breast and prostate cancer.

His work in gastrointestinal cancers is co-managed with Dr Choo SP, a GI Medical Oncologist from Curie Singapore. Before Curie Oncology KL, he was Head of Oncology and Clinical Research at Aurelius Hospital Nilai (2021–2023), and practised at Institut Kanser Negara (2020–2021) and Sabah Women and Children Hospital (2019–2020).

Cancer types managed
