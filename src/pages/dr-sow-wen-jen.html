About Dr Sow Wen Jen

Dr Sow Wen Jen (Jenson) focuses on the treatment of gastrointestinal cancers. He completed his MBBS at the International Medical University (IMU) in 2008, and later earned his Master of Clinical Oncology from Universiti Malaya Medical Centre (UMMC) in 2018. He was certified as a Clinical Oncologist under the National Specialist Registry (NSR) in December 2020.

His career began with a house officer internship in 2010, followed by a posting as Medical Officer at the Oncology Division of Hospital Kuala Lumpur in 2012. After completing his Master's, he served as Clinical Oncologist at Sabah Women and Children's Hospital before transferring to Institut Kanser Negara in June 2020.

Dr Sow is a committee member of the Malaysian Oncological Society, which he has belonged to since 2013, and holds memberships with the European Society for Medical Oncology (ESMO), the European Society for Radiotherapy and Oncology (ESTRO), and the International Atomic Energy Agency (IAEA). In 2019 he received the "Young Newcomers in Oncology Clinical Research" accolade, and has contributed to Pan-Asian Guideline Adaptation projects in metastatic colorectal cancer and biliary tract cancer.
