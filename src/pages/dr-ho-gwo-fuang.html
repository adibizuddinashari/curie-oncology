About Dr Ho Gwo Fuang

Dr Ho is a Senior Consultant Clinical Oncologist treating cancer with chemotherapy, targeted therapy, immunotherapy and radiotherapy. He has practised at consultant level since 2005, including 17 years at Universiti Malaya Medical Centre.

As Professor at Universiti Malaya, he trained new oncologists in Malaysia and headed the Clinical Oncology Unit (2021–2023). His specialist training was at Barts and The London and The Royal Marsden NHS Trusts in the UK.

He is the author of the patient books Cancer and You (2015) and Written for Cancer Patient (2014).

Cancer types managed

Lung, gastrointestinal, hepatobiliary and breast cancers.

Clinical interests

  • Co-author, Pan-Asian adapted ESMO guidelines for hepatocellular carcinoma (2020)
  • ASCO expert panel, resource-stratified colorectal cancer guidelines (2019)
