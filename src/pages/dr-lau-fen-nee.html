About Dr Lau Fen Nee

Dr Lau Fen Nee brings nearly three decades of experience from Malaysia's Ministry of Health to Curie Oncology. She obtained her medical degree (MBBS) from Universiti Malaya in 1991, and went on to earn her Fellowship from the Royal College of Radiologists (Clinical Oncology, UK) in 1999.

Her career has spanned University Hospital Kuala Lumpur, Hospital Sultanah Aminah, and the Institute of Radiotherapy and Oncology, with a visiting specialist registrar attachment at the Royal Marsden Hospital, UK, in 1996. She went on to serve as Clinical Oncologist, and later Head of the Department of Radiotherapy and Oncology, at Institut Kanser Negara (National Cancer Institute).

Beyond clinical practice, Dr Lau lectures on Universiti Malaya's Master of Clinical Oncology and Medical Physics programmes, and has chaired both the Specialty Subcommittee for Clinical Oncology at the National Specialist Register and the FRCR (Clinical Oncology) Parallel Pathway Committee. From 2014 to 2021, she led radiation oncology initiatives through the Forum for Nuclear Cooperation in Asia.
