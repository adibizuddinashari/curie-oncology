About Dr Lau Fen Nee

Dr Lau spent 30 years in Malaysia’s Ministry of Health hospitals before joining Curie Oncology, 22 of them as a consultant.

She joined Institut Kanser Negara (National Cancer Institute) as a pioneer consultant in 2013 and headed its Department of Radiotherapy and Oncology (2020–2021). Before that she was a consultant at the Institute of Radiotherapy and Oncology, Hospital Kuala Lumpur (1999–2013), following a Visiting Specialist Registrar post at the Royal Marsden Hospital, UK (1996–1999).

She has trained Malaysia’s clinical oncologists for over 20 years, teaching radiation biology, clinical oncology and the Advanced Diploma in CT for therapy radiographers, and has been a lecturer, supervisor and examiner for the Master of Clinical Oncology at Universiti Malaya since 2002.
