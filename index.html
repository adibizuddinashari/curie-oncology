Who We Are

A regional oncology practice, specialising in complex oncology. We provide care, in a multi-disciplinary role, that is specific and personalised, helping patients overcome cancer, and rebuild their lives.

“

Our mission at Curie Oncology is to advance cancer care to its best version. By integrating precision genomics, advanced clinical trials, and innovative therapeutics, we strive to deliver the full promise of modern science to every patient we serve.

”

Dr John Chia (Co-Founder)

Our Mission

  • In an era of medical and technological breakthroughs, we bring science to the clinic to cure cancer and restore health.
  • Our philosophy: accurately diagnose, understand, and personalise care through precision medicine and molecular imaging.
  • Using immunotherapy, chemotherapy, and targeted agents, we detect and treat cancer early to maximise every patient's chance of cure.

Our Values

  • Compassion First - we care for each patient as if they were our own family; every journey is personal, and every story matters.
  • Innovation with Purpose - we adopt breakthrough technologies early, giving patients access to the most advanced diagnostics and treatments available.
  • Driven by Research - studies that improve diagnosis, treatment, and prevention, toward a future free from the fear of cancer.
  • Rebuilding Lives - beyond treatment, we help patients reclaim their health, strength, and future.
