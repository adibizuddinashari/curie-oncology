About Dato' Dr Fuad Ismail

Dato' Dr Fuad Ismail is Professor and Head of the Department of Radiotherapy & Oncology at Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia Medical Centre (UKMMC), and joins Curie Oncology as a Visiting Senior Consultant Clinical Oncologist. His clinical focus spans breast, cervical, and colorectal cancers.

He earned his MD from Universiti Kebangsaan Malaysia in 1990, followed by his FFRRCSI from the Royal College of Surgeons in Ireland and his FRCR from the Royal College of Radiologists, London - both in 1996. He has previously practised as an Oncologist at UKM Medical Centre and Gleneagles Hospital Kuala Lumpur.

Dato' Dr Fuad is an active committee member of the Malaysian Oncological Society and has served as National Coordinator for the Regional Cooperative Agreement Project with the International Atomic Energy Agency, alongside roles on multiple Ministry of Health cancer committees. He lectures widely on radiotherapy, chemotherapy, and cancer management, and has authored numerous publications and served as Principal Investigator or Collaborator across several oncology research projects.
